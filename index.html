Prism Siege – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Short version: Prism Siege has no accounts and does not sell your information. Your progress is saved on your own device. The game shows optional reward videos from Google AdMob, which uses a device advertising ID; in the EU and UK you are asked for consent first and can change your choice at any time.

Who we are

Prism Siege ("the game") is developed by dithegav. If you have any question about this policy, contact us at dithegav@gmail.com.

Information the game collects

The game itself has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. It does not access your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.

Optional reward videos (Google AdMob)

You can choose to watch a short video for an in-game reward (for example a free chest, double shards or a revive). Videos are never shown unless you tap a button that asks for one. To show these videos the game uses Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and approximate location, and information about the ads you see and interact with, to deliver, measure and (if you agree) personalise ads, and to prevent fraud. This is handled by Google under its own policy:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Your choices

  • In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland the game shows Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can accept, refuse, or manage your choices. If you refuse, you still get the videos, but ads are not personalised.
  • You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → AD PRIVACY OPTIONS.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • If you never tap a video button, no video is shown.

Data stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves small files on your device containing game data only: levels reached, stars, upgrades, in-game currencies, style items, Prism Pass progress, settings and statistics such as enemies defeated. These files never leave your device. Uninstalling the game, or using Settings → Reset all progress, erases them.

Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information under its own privacy policy when you download or update apps (for example crash reports you agree to share with developers). See the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that use data (for example cloud saves through Google Play Games, or in-app purchases through Google Play), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
