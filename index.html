Prism Siege – Privacy Policy Last updated: 24 October 2026 Short version: Prism Siege does not sell your information. Your progress is saved on your own device (and, if you use Google Play Games, in your Play Games cloud save). For the world leaderboards the game keeps the player name you choose, a random player ID and your scores on our server. The game shows optional reward videos from Google AdMob, which uses a device advertising ID; in the EU and UK you are asked for consent first and can change your choice at any time. Who we are Prism Siege ("the game") is developed by dithegav. If you have any question about this policy, contact us at dithegav@gmail.com. Information the game collects The game does not ask for an email or a password, and does not access your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone. World leaderboards and player name (our server) To rank players against each other, the game uses its own online server (Google Firebase, run by Google in the European Union, Belgium region). When the game connects it creates an anonymous player account (a random ID, no email or password) and stores: the player name you choose in the game, your leaderboard scores (best Endless Rift waves, player level, campaign stars and stars earned today and this week), and, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, your Play Games player ID, so your name and records follow you to a new phone. Other players can see your player name and scores on the leaderboards. This data is sent encrypted, is used only for the leaderboards and is never sold or shared for advertising. Past daily and weekly lists stay stored with your scores but are no longer shown in the game. To have your player name and leaderboard data deleted, email dithegav@gmail.com with your player name; we delete it within 30 days. Google Play Games (sign-in, cloud save, Google leaderboards) If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game saves a copy of your progress in your Play Games cloud save (so you can continue on another phone) and sends your records to Google's Play Games leaderboards. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in. In-app purchases Optional purchases (gems, the Prism Pass, skins, ad-free rewards) are processed by Google Play. The game never sees your payment details; it only receives a confirmation of what was bought. Anonymous gameplay statistics (GameAnalytics) To find out where the game is too hard, too easy or confusing, the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics to GameAnalytics ApS (Denmark): which levels you start, win or lose, tutorial steps, in-game currency earned and spent, reward videos watched, purchases (product and price only), crash reports (what the game was doing, no personal data), and your device model, Android version and country. These are linked only to a random ID created by the game, not to your name, email or advertising ID. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → ANONYMOUS STATS. GameAnalytics policy: gameanalytics.com/privacy. Optional reward videos (Google AdMob) You can choose to watch a short video for an in-game reward (for example a free chest, double shards or a revive). Videos are never shown unless you tap a button that asks for one. To show these videos the game uses Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and approximate location, and information about the ads you see and interact with, to deliver, measure and (if you agree) personalise ads, and to prevent fraud. This is handled by Google under its own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services Google Privacy Policy Your choices In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland the game shows Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can accept, refuse, or manage your choices. If you refuse, you still get the videos, but ads are not personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → AD PRIVACY OPTIONS. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). If you never tap a video button, no video is shown. Data stored on your device To remember your progress, the game saves small files on your device containing game data only: levels reached, stars, upgrades, in-game currencies, style items, Prism Pass progress, settings and statistics such as enemies defeated. Apart from the Play Games cloud save and the leaderboard data described above, they stay on your device. Uninstalling the game, or using Settings → Reset all progress, erases them. Google Play The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information under its own privacy policy when you download or update apps (for example crash reports you agree to share with developers). See the Google Privacy Policy. Children The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. Changes to this policy If a future version uses data in a new way (for example online leagues with other players), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
